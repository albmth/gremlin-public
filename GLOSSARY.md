# Glossary

**Imprint**: one generation: a reference identity transferred into a driving clip.

**Reference**: the photo of who goes into the clip.

**Driving clip**: the video or animated sticker that supplies the motion, timing, camera and scene.

**Target**: the one character in the driving clip that gets replaced; everyone and everything else stays.

**Tier**: one of two accepted recipes the client picks per job: `simple-motion` (distilled, 8 steps) or `complex-motion` (guided, 12 steps).

**Session**: one GPU worker run on RunPod; it serves queued jobs one at a time and is reserved and settled as money.

**Accepted output**: a fixture output the owner reviewed as video and kept as the quality reference; technically fine is not accepted.

**Direct and delegated client**: a direct client is one scope; a delegated client is a backend that scopes jobs per end user with `X-Gremlin-Owner`.
